# btc-5m-predictor
Simple Binance-based BTC prediction bot that forecasts whether price will move UP or DOWN over the next 5-minute interval using market microstructure signals like order book imbalance, trade pressure, VWAP deviation, volume spikes, volatility, and tick momentum.

This bot is experimental and for research purposes only. Short-term crypto price prediction (especially 5-minute intervals) is highly noisy and often close to random. The bot may perform poorly and should not be used for serious financial decisions.
